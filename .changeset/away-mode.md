Add opt-in away mode (`away.mode`: `"off"` | `"auto"` | `"always"`, off by default; with it off nothing changes). Prompts that nobody answers no longer hold a run: in `auto` a prompt waits `away.promptTimeoutMs` (2 minutes) and then you count as away; in `always` nothing prompts. While away, an action the delegates can't settle is run and logged when it is low risk, or queued for you and not run. Also adds repeat handling, a tripwire for severe blocks, a watch window after blocks, stuck and limit handling with pause and resume, advisors (`guardrails:advisor:register`), standing permissions (`away.standingPermissions`), plain-language cards, high-risk allows made while away listed in the inbox for review, a machine-wide inbox with `/guardrails:inbox`, `/guardrails:away`, pi-live inbox items and prompt deadlines (`expiresAt`, `by: "timeout"`), and a new `away` extension. Delegate answers gain optional `risk`, `severe` and `card`, and requests gain `watch` and `standingPermissions`. New `permissionGate.allowSafeCleanup` option (default `false`) stops `rm -rf` of known build and cache folders inside the project from being flagged. `delegation.timeoutMs` is now capped at 300000 ms instead of 60000 ms.
